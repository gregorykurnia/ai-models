# Model app logo exploration

These marks are deliberately less literal than the first `icon-options` set. They avoid the usual dashboard shorthand of bars, lists, charts, and check marks, and instead give the product one recognisable silhouette at a glance.

Open [index.html](./index.html) to view them in a gallery and at small app-icon sizes.

## Directions

1. **Contour** — a model landscape with a chosen reference point. Best fit for **Model Atlas**.
2. **Facet** — inputs resolved into distinct planes. Best fit for **Model Index**.
3. **Weave** — evidence streams crossing into one shared path. Best fit for a product that foregrounds comparisons.
4. **Beacon** — signal discovered and made visible. Best fit for **Model Signals**.
5. **Orbit** — models, evaluations, and tasks in relation. The most expansive direction.
6. **Delta** — a compact upward form, with an abstract A silhouette. Best for a tiny browser or app icon.

My strongest starting point is **Contour**. It is specific to the Atlas idea, has a memorable silhouette, and stays readable when reduced.
