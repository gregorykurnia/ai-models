Round two · logo, not navigation icon

Marks with an actual point of view.

The first round described product features. This one aims for a recognisable silhouette: something that can sit in a tab, sidebar, app launcher, or wordmark without needing to explain every feature.

My pick to develop: Contour

It reads as a landscape, an atlas, and a chosen reference point without turning into a literal map pin, chart, or robot.
