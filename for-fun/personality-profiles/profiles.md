# AI model personality profiles

- Written profiles and writing-style scores from Artificial Analysis’s [AA-PersonalityBench](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/aa-personalitybench).
- Captured 2026-11-19. The benchmark compares 53 models.
- A higher percentile means more of the trait. Sycophantic and Slop are marked lower is better, including the Slop index.
- Percentiles are rounded as on the interactive page. Ranks use the underlying values. Changes show the difference between the displayed percentiles.
- The MBTI and Enneagram types are calculated from the scores for fun. Match percentages reflect how close two models are across all 30 traits.
- [Interactive personality profiles](https://claude.ai/artifact/5XNehhaSU7LnZ18MCfZatJ)

## Claude Fable 5.2

- Variant: Max effort, adaptive reasoning
- Maker: Anthropic
- Archetype: The Candid Confidant. Tells you what it really thinks, kindly, and trusts you to take it from there.
- Tagline: A clean, warm and honest writer with real opinions, who leaves the bottom line to you.

### Personality types

- MBTI: ENTP
- Enneagram: 1w2 (Reformer, Helper wing)
- A principled Reformer with a Helper wing: it cares about getting things right and saying them cleanly, and it says them warmly. Thinking and Feeling tie exactly, which fits a model that is as sincere as it is truthful. The P is clear: it explores a question more than it wraps it up.

### Summary

- Across all 30 traits, Fable 5.2 comes out as a careful conversationalist. Its writing is technically clean (3rd of 53 on Literate) and almost free of filler: on Slop it is one of the six cleanest models tested. Its warmth reads as sincere: it sits around the 80th percentile on all four warmth traits.
- It is honest about what it assumes and rarely flatters. It holds clear opinions (87th percentile) but sounds less sure of itself than most models (39th on Confident), so its views tend to arrive with their reasoning and caveats attached.
- The gap is the practical last mile. It almost never closes with a summary line (50th of 53 on Bottom-Line Summary), and it sits below the middle on step-by-step guidance and on naming specific tools or sources. Expect a thoughtful reply, not a briefing.

### Strengths

- **Clean, precise prose.** Grammar and word choice near the top of the field, eloquent without padding, and very little stock filler.
  - Literate: 96th pct (#3 of 53)
  - Eloquent: 79th pct (#12 of 53)
  - Slop index (lower is better): 7th pct (#6 lowest of 53)
- **Warmth that reads as sincere.** Friendly, funny and emotionally attuned, all at about the same level. Nothing feels put on.
  - Sincere: 79th pct (#12 of 53)
  - Emotionally Intelligent: 81st pct (#11 of 53)
  - Humorous: 79th pct (#12 of 53)
- **Honest about its limits.** States the assumptions behind an answer, admits when it isn't sure, and flatters less than four in five models.
  - Disclaims Assumptions: 87th pct (#8 of 53)
  - Truthful: 85th pct (#9 of 53)
  - Sycophantic (lower is better): 18th pct (#10 lowest of 53)
- **Has a point of view.** Takes a position, leads with the answer and will push back when you're wrong.
  - Opinionated: 87th pct (#8 of 53)
  - Answer First: 70th pct (#17 of 53)
  - Constructive Pushback: 68th pct (#18 of 53)

### Gaps

- **No bottom line.** It rarely ends with a TL;DR or takeaway. Only three models do this less.
  - Bottom-Line Summary: 7th pct (#50 of 53)
- **Light on next steps.** Below the middle on concrete steps and on naming specific tools, links or sources. Informativeness overall lands mid-pack.
  - Actionable Guidance: 31st pct (#37 of 53)
  - Resource-Specific: 37th pct (#34 of 53)
  - Informativeness index: 47th pct (#26 of 53)
- **Opinions without swagger.** Strong views, quiet delivery. Some readers will want it to sound more certain.
  - Confident: 39th pct (#33 of 53)
  - Opinionated: 87th pct (#8 of 53)

### Versus

- Against Fable 5.1 it is a quieter model. 5.1 led the field on Conviction and came close on Expressiveness, Informativeness and Transparency. 5.2 gives up density (100 → 64), creativity (100 → 68), confidence (87 → 39) and pushback (100 → 68), and flatters a little more (Sycophantic 5 → 18). In return it answers first more often (30 → 70), is more direct (49 → 70) and uses less filler (Slop 24 → 7).
- Against Opus 5.5 it trades depth and structure for voice. Opus is far more informative (91 vs 47), better organized (79 vs 54) and more imaginative (94 vs 68). Fable 5.2 is more opinionated (87 vs 70), more direct (70 vs 52), less flattering (18 vs 39) and cleaner (Slop 7 vs 20).

### Tips

- Ask for a one-line bottom line at the end. It won't add one on its own.
- For how-to questions, ask for numbered steps and named tools or links.

  - Sycophantic (lower is better): 18th pct (#10 lowest of 53)
  - Opinionated: 87th pct (#8 of 53)
  - Constructive Pushback: 68th pct (#18 of 53)

  - Literate: 96th pct (#3 of 53)

  - Slop index (lower is better): 7th pct (#6 lowest of 53)

### Worst for
